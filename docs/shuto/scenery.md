# Shuto rep section: scenery (buildings, water, props)

Status as of 2026-10-10. Scope: bbox x -2600..0, z 1500..6000 (NOX frame, metres), 500 m chunks.
Generated by `tools/shuto/scenery_plan.py` (Python, stdlib) and
`tools/shuto/blender/build_scenery.py` (Blender 4.3 headless). Road geometry is unchanged
(`models/shuto/rep_section/render`, `collision`, `chunks.json`).

## Commands

```
python -I tools/shuto/scenery_plan.py            # OSM fetch (cached) + plan
"C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Blender\blender.exe" -b --factory-startup ^
  --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/shuto/blender/build_scenery.py -- ^
  --plan models/shuto/rep_section/scenery_plan.json --out models/shuto/rep_section
```

Outputs (models/ is git-ignored):

| Path | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `models/shuto/rep_section/scenery/<cx>_<cz>.glb` | render meshes, world coordinates, 54 chunk files (blank chunks omitted) |
| `models/shuto/rep_section/scenery_collision/<cx>_<cz>_col.glb` | building walls (+ roofs), landmark boxes, container walls |
| `models/shuto/rep_section/scenery_plan.json` | planner output (water rectangles, buildings, landmarks, containers, trees) |
| `models/shuto/rep_section/scenery.json` | chunk list, triangle totals, materials, textures, placements, attribution |
| `models/shuto/rep_section/scenery_tex/` | 1024 px facade base and lit-window atlases, roof, foliage (derived) |
| `models/shuto/src/rep_section_scenery.blend` | editable scene (all render and collision objects) |
| `data/shuto/raw/overpass_scenery_<kind>_<tile>.json` | cached raw Overpass responses (ODbL) |

Chunk index: `cx = floor(x / 500)`, `cz = floor(z / 500)`, same as `chunks.json`.
Chunk assignment of an object is by centroid. Objects that cross a chunk edge are
stored in world coordinates, so they render correctly from either chunk.

## Geometry rules

- **Water**: a 4 m raster over the bbox. Sea = coastline-enclosed flood-fill components on the
  side OSM marks as sea (right of the way). Natural=water and riverbank polygons and river/canal
  lines are added. Water under at-grade road corridors (deck y < 4 m) is removed.
  Rectangles are merged and split at chunk edges.
  Material `water`: base colour (0.05, 0.09, 0.12), roughness 0.05, metallic 0.
  **Height is y = 3.05, not 1.0.** The existing ground gravel sits at ground_y - 0.05 = 2.95
  and the road ground is 3.0. Water at 1.0 would be hidden under the ground plane, so the
  water surface is placed just above it, clipped to the water raster. Bridges over water stay
  clear because their decks are above 3.05 m.
- **Carriageway bands**: per carriageway, the band half-width is
  lanes * lane_width / 2 + shoulder_left + shoulder_right + 4 m (BAND_MARGIN). Bands are
  tested at any height. A footprint conflicts if its boundary (1.5 m samples) comes within
  band + 1.5 m planning slack of a centreline segment, or a centreline point lies inside it.
  This covers footprints that a deck passes through, not only those it touches.
- **Buildings**: OSM ways and multipolygon outers with centroid in the bbox. Dropped if
  the footprint conflicts with a band (25 dropped), or the footprint touches water. Height = `height` tag, else `building:levels` x 3.3 m,
  else a default by family (commercial 30 m, apartment 16 m, industrial 10 m).
  Facade family: `building` tag, then height/position (industrial when z > 3000 for untagged).
  Base y = 2.9 (just under the gravel).
  - **Full LOD** (2795 buildings within 350 m of an expressway, within the triangle budget):
    one quad per bay x storey, bay 3.5 m (apartment), 4.0 m (commercial), 6.0 m (industrial),
    storey 3.3 m. Each quad maps the full patch rectangle (0..1 in the patch), so the
    clamp-only atlas never tiles. Roofs use rooftop_membrane (UV in 4 m tiles).
  - **Plain LOD** (4680): one quad per edge with the same facade atlas patch (UV over the
    full edge, same triangle count as before), plus a rooftop_membrane roof.
  - Night albedo: facade atlases are darkened to a mean sRGB of 0.40 (about 0.13 linear), and
    the roof membrane to 0.26 sRGB. Both were measured in the build log.
  - Emissive windows: `facades_*_lit.jpg` (768 px) marks dark/blue-grey glass blocks (24 px
    cells). Lit cells are 25 to 30% of candidate cells, 23 to 36% of them cool white and the
    rest warm. Texture value is 0.75 times the colour. The material uses
    KHR_materials_emissive_strength 12.0 (x3 of the first pass, which was 4.0).
- **Landmarks**: 120 kit buildings, each scaled to its OBB (footprint width x depth, height
  from OSM), yaw aligned to the footprint's longest edge. Collision is an OBB box.
  The full OBB rectangle must clear every band (2 candidates dropped). Height is capped at
  120 m, except within 350 m of the Shiodome centre (-1130, 2425), where the cap is 250 m.
  Four landmarks exceed 120 m (max 215.8 m), all inside that radius and justified by OSM heights.
  Before the fix, a kit landmark reached 211 m across Hamazakibashi, so the cap was added.
- **Containers** (footprint rectangle clear of every band): 46 stacks (1 or 2 high, 12.19 x 2.44 x 2.59 m) on industrial landuse, away
  from roads and water. Material `container`.
- **Trees** (centre at least 3.5 m outside every band): 882 crossed foliage cards (4 to 6.5 m) in parks, at about 22% of jittered
  12 m cells. Shrub, hedge, grass or weed cell from `foliage_cutouts`. Material `foliage` is
  alpha MASK at cutoff 0.5 and double sided. Blender 4.3 exports this as BLEND, so
  `build_scenery.py` rewrites the JSON chunk to MASK after export.
- **Collision**: walls for every building (both LODs, no roofs), landmark OBB boxes, container
  walls. Material `collision`. Triangles are 76,958 in total.

## Validation

`python -I tools/shuto/check_scenery.py` loads every scenery collision GLB, applies node
translations, and asserts that no wall triangle lies within any carriageway band (exact band
width, no planning slack). Triangle edges are sampled every 0.5 m, plus vertices and centroid.
It reports offenders and exits 1 if any exist. Last run: PASS, 76,804 triangles, 48 files.
A positive control confirmed that a centreline point is detected and a point 400 m away is not.

## Materials (contract with the engine)

`facade_apartment`, `facade_commercial`, `facade_industrial` (base + emissive, used by full
and far masses), `roof_membrane`, `container`, `foliage` (MASK), `water`, `collision`, plus the kit's own materials
(`BACKGROUND_BUILDINGS_1`, `BACKGROUND_BUILDING_2`) on landmark meshes.
The NOX loader does not need KHR_texture_transform. Only KHR_materials_emissive_strength
and clearcoat (kit) appear as extensions.

## Known gaps

- **Water edges are stair-stepped** at 4 m, a flood-fill approximation, not a smooth coastline
  polygon. Reef and river mouth detail is approximate.
- **Water versus roads**: water is removed only under at-grade decks (y < 4), using the
  band width. A deck above 4 m over water is not cut, so water shows beneath bridges.
- **Piers**: build_roads places piers on the carriageway centreline (+-0.9 m), so they lie
  inside every band and are covered by the band test. Piers are not read separately.
- **Relations**: only outer rings are used. Holes (courtyards) are filled, so a few inner
  courts are solid.
- **Heights**: 89% of kept buildings (6662 of 7476) have no `height` tag. Most heights are
  family defaults (`building:levels` covers some of them), so the skyline is coarse. Shiodome towers are tall via OSM tags (up to ~193 m near the cluster).
- **Lit windows** are block-based, not per window. The emissive mask is derived from a dark
  blue/grey heuristic, so some non-glass areas can light up.
- **Facade variety**: 4 patches per family, chosen per building by a deterministic index.
  Bay and storey repetition is regular.
- **Far buildings** are untextured masses, so the skyline beyond 350 m reads as flat colour.
- **Containers** may intersect buildings. No footprint test was run for them.
- **Trees** are in parks only. Street trees are not placed.
- **Japanese kit** is excluded. No layer file was produced.
- **Kit textures** are reduced to 256 px, which may look soft on the nearest landmarks.
- **Not visually verified in NOX.** The GLBs were checked by parsing (bounds, materials,
  alpha mode, emissive extension). Rendering has not been reviewed.
