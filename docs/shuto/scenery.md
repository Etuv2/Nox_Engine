# Shuto rep section: scenery (buildings, water, props)

Status as of 2026-10-10 (facade units added in pass 2; see the section below). Scope: bbox x -2600..0, z 1500..6000 (NOX frame, metres), 500 m chunks.
Generated by `tools/shuto/scenery_plan.py` (Python, stdlib) and
`tools/shuto/blender/build_scenery.py` (Blender 4.3 headless). Road geometry is unchanged
(`models/shuto/rep_section/render`, `collision`, `chunks.json`).

## City pass 2 and 3 (2026-10-10)

Pipeline (the planner and builder are the only writers of `models/shuto/rep_section/scenery*`):

```
python -I tools/shuto/make_facade_units.py     # textures/shuto/derived/facade_units/ (PIL, numpy, scipy)
python -I tools/shuto/scenery_plan.py          # OSM buildings, streets (new "streets" kind), zones, flags
"<blender>" -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/shuto/blender/build_scenery.py -- --plan models/shuto/rep_section/scenery_plan.json --out models/shuto/rep_section
python -I tools/shuto/check_scenery.py
python -I tools/shuto/build_scene.py --section rep_section
```

**Facades**

- Units: each unit is one storey by one bay (3.5 m apartment, 4.0 m commercial, 6.0 m industrial)
  at the measured storey pitch of its cell. Units are packed with 8 px edge padding. Mean sRGB
  albedo is capped at 0.40.
- Full LOD (2008 buildings within 350 m of an expressway): one quad per bay x storey, each taking one
  unit. Bottom storey uses ground units (shopfronts, lit more often).
- Far LOD (the rest, 3120 buildings): a ground quad from the ground units, then one quad per bay with
  V REPEATing a single-storey far tile (`facades_<fam>_far<k>.png`, 3 per family). No stretching,
  and far masses stay cheap. Tile seams show at close range.
- Lit windows: each glass component (area >= 400 px) is lit on its own (16% upper, 45% ground),
  warm or cool, emissive strength 7. Lit density still reads high at close range (see captures).
- Towers (h >= 60 m): setback at 70% of the height, upper volume at 72% of the footprint (inside it).
- Parapet lip (cornice) on every building, roof material per family (rooftop_membrane, or
  corrugated_metal on waterfront industrial), and simple rooftop boxes: AC units, a water tank,
  a stair tower (roof membrane).
- Signage: billboard-art panel above the shopfronts on Ginza commercial buildings (scenery only).

**Districts** (approximate regions, from the sprint waypoints and the Shiodome cluster):

| Zone | Rule | Untagged buildings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ginza | within 700 m of (-425, 1657), z < 2300 | commercial, 8 to 12 storeys |
| Shiodome | within 350 m of (-1130, 2425) | commercial, 14 to 26 storeys |
| Waterfront | water within 60 m, z > 3000 | industrial warehouses, 5 to 13 m, corrugated roofs |
| Daiba | z > 4800, within 900 m of (-700, 5300) | commercial or apartment, 6 to 16 storeys |
| Hamamatsucho / Shibaura | everything else | apartment (< 350 m2) or commercial, 6 to 13 storeys |

Tagged heights (`height`, `building:levels`) are always used.

**Streets**

- OSM `highway` = primary, secondary, tertiary and residential inside the bbox. Pieces are split
  where they meet an expressway band or water. Widths: 14, 11, 9 and 7 m, with sidewalks of 3.0,
  2.5, 2.2 and 1.8 m. Asphalt at y 3.03, sidewalks at 3.15, kerbs between them.
- Paint: yellow double centre line on primary, white dashed centre on secondary, white edge lines
  on primary and secondary (`textures/road_decals`).
- Street trees every 14 m along the sidewalks (foliage cards). Street lamps every 30 m on primary
  and secondary: a pole box and an emissive head box (no engine lights).
- Buildings within the street clearance (road half-width + sidewalk + 0.3 m) are dropped (2,330).
- Crosswalks, junction geometry and road-level traffic are not included.

**Budgets (measured, 2026-10-10)**

- Scenery render triangles 1,025,924 (roads are separate, about 0.67 M), so about 1.7 M in total.
- Collision triangles 53,638 (`check_scenery.py` PASS).
- Scenery GLBs 333 MB render + 5.4 MB collision (budget 350 MB total; close to the limit).
- Performance, 3440x1440 borderless High, 9000-frame autopilot run: summed pass GPU time per
  frame, after frame 600, 70 samples, median 3.18 ms, p95 4.53 ms (max 11.6 ms). This sums pass
  timings and is not the whole-frame figure reported before, so the two are not comparable.

**Kit landmarks**: unchanged from pass 1 (120 asian_themed landmarks, scaled to their footprint,
not stretched). The placement review was not completed.

**Not done**

- Shopfront units distinct from the upper units (ground units are lit more often but reuse
  the same facade cells).
- Plinth, district-specific balcony bands on Hamamatsucho apartments, Daiba waterfront detail.
- Street lights reuse no road-agent asset (no lamp GLB is shipped); the lamp is modelled simply.
- Close-range facade quality: repeating far tiles show seams, and lit windows are dense.
- Camera poses used for captures are approximate. The Shiodome ground view looks across a canal.

**Interior mapping (investigated, not used).** `textures/window_rooms` needs a renderer change.
`shaders/includes/interior_mapping.glsl` draws rooms procedurally, and `gbuffer_frag.glsl`
skips the emissive texture while interior mapping is on. An imported glTF material cannot
enable it from the scene JSON, so the lit windows are emissive. Enabling it needs a generic
engine change to sample the five-face atlas with `atlas_manifest.json` rectangles.

## Facade styles (pass 4, 2026-10-10)

Buildings now use one coherent window design per style instead of per-quad unit cut-outs.

```
python -I tools/shuto/make_facade_styles.py    # textures/shuto/derived/facade_styles/ (PIL, numpy, scipy)
python -I tools/shuto/scenery_plan.py          # per building: style, tint (0..2), offset (bay, storey)
"<blender>" -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/shuto/blender/build_scenery.py -- --plan models/shuto/rep_section/scenery_plan.json --out models/shuto/rep_section
python -I tools/shuto/check_scenery.py
python -I tools/shuto/build_scene.py --section rep_section
```

- **12 styles** (`manifest.json`): spandrel_midrise, office_ribbon, curtain_mullion, curtain_bronze,
  apartment_balcony, apartment_concrete, tile_older, ivory_modern, warehouse_clerestory,
  warehouse_roller, shopfront_retail, dark_office. Each has `<name>_base.jpg` (wall plus glass,
  frames, sills, spandrels or balcony rails) and `<name>_emit.jpg` (per-window lit state).
- **Wall colour** comes from the cleanest window-free crop of the supplied patches (facades_*
  cells, concrete_cast, concrete_weathered, corrugated_metal). The crop is mirror-tiled
  (A | flip(A) in both axes), so it is seamless. Windows are drawn on top.
- **Texture size**: 1024 px per style, covering **16 bays x 12 storeys**. This is smaller than the
  2048 px brief, to keep the GLB budget.
- **Lit state**: 25 % of windows lit (warm 70 %, cool 30 %). Some are partly lit (blinds, 35 %
  intensity, 10 %). About 5 % of storeys are dark. Measured emissive area is 4 to 16 % of pixels,
  with the window glass covering the rest.
- **Mapping**: one quad per wall edge (see pass 5 for the current sizes and textures). The wall starts on a whole bay (`u0 = off_bay / 16`) and
  spans `round(L / bay_m)` bays. Height spans `round(h / storey_m)` storeys from `off_storey`.
  Storey rows therefore line up on every wall of a building. Texture REPEAT is on, with mips.
  Bay and storey stretch is at most about 20 %.
- **Choice**: by district and family (scenery_plan.py `pick_style`). Ginza uses spandrel, ribbon
  or tile. Shiodome uses curtain walls at 60 m or more, otherwise ribbon. Waterfront uses
  warehouse. Daiba uses balcony or ribbon. Hamamatsucho uses balcony, concrete or tile
  for apartments and ribbon for offices.
- **Tint**: 3 material variants per style (`style_<name>_t<0..2>`), a multiply on the base colour.
  This is a material parameter, not a new texture.
- **Near buildings** (within 250 m of the expressway, 1,417 of 5,128): shopfront ground floor
  (`shopfront_retail`, one storey, 0.36 emissive area), cornice lip, rooftop boxes (AC units,
  tank, stair tower), balcony slabs per storey on apartment styles (boxes, longest wall only),
  and billboard signs on Ginza and Shiodome buildings.
- **Towers** (60 m or more): setback at 70 % of height, upper volume at 72 % of the footprint.
- **Far buildings** get the same wall quad with no near detail, so far LOD costs one quad per wall.

**Budgets (measured, 2026-10-10)**: scenery render triangles 307,364 (roads separate, about 0.67 M),
collision 53,638 triangles, scenery GLBs 318 MB plus 5.4 MB collision (budget about 350 MB).
`check_scenery.py` PASS. The GLB size is dominated by 1024 px style textures embedded per chunk.

**Known gaps**: the expressway view at 200 to 800 m shows large lit panels up close, because a
storey cell is about 3.5 m. Window texels are coarse at 1024 px. Shopfront signage is a single
billboard, not per-shop. Kit landmarks are unchanged from pass 1 (true scale, original
textures). The landmark placement review is not done.

## Pass 5 (2026-10-11): room-painted panes, external textures

- **Styles at 2048 px** (16 bays x 12 storeys, `make_facade_styles.py`, 9.9 MB on disk).
- **Lit panes show rooms**: each lit pane takes a random crop (55 to 100 %, optional mirror) of the
  back wall of a `textures/window_rooms` atlas, desaturated 30 %. Tints: warm (70 %), cool (30 %),
  and the dim TV room in blue (only one of six rooms). 30 % of lit panes have drawn blinds (slats
  over 30 to 85 % of the pane). The emissive map holds the room content at 0.9. The base colour
  is the same content at 0.22 (dark glass). Unlit panes keep dark glass with a faint reflection.
- **Emission strength 2.5** (build `EMIT_STRENGTH`). Engine exposure EV100 3 is not set by this
  pipeline, so this value is a starting point.
- **External textures**: render chunks export as `.gltf` (`GLTF_SEPARATE`), one `.bin` per chunk,
  and every texture is written once into `models/shuto/rep_section/scenery/` with relative URIs.
  `build_scene.py` globs `scenery/*.gltf`. Collision stays `.glb`. The NOX loader resolves the
  relative URIs (verified in-engine: textures appear, no load errors).
- **Size**: the scenery folder is now 49 MB, down from 318 MB (about 40 unique JPG/PNG textures
  plus 64 `.gltf` and 64 `.bin` chunks). Triangles are unchanged at 307,364.
- **Duplicate uploads (engine issue)**: the load log reports `textureRefs=2280, uniqueTextureLoads=2037`
  across 120 model loads, with `materialCacheHits=71` and `materialCacheMisses=1383`. The model
  cache does not share textures across chunk files, so each texture is uploaded once per chunk
  that uses it. That is about 50 times the unique count. Fixing it needs a ModelManager change
  (cache keyed by resolved file URI). Not changed here.
- **Expressway pose fixed**: the previous pose (-1200, 22, 2350) was 93 m from the nearest carriageway,
  floating above the Shiodome streets. The new pose is on the deck of C1.CCW.023 (-1306.45, 22.4,
  2680.1), looking along the lane. The nearest building is 155 m away. Confirmed in
  `captures/shuto/city_v5/expressway_view.png` (deck, parapets, lane paint, lamps; no wall beside the lane).

## Geometry rules

- **Water**: a 4 m raster over the bbox. Sea = coastline-enclosed flood-fill components on the
  side OSM marks as sea (right of the way). Natural=water and riverbank polygons and river/canal
  lines are added. Water under at-grade road corridors (deck y < 4 m) is removed.
  Rectangles are merged and split at chunk edges.
  Material `water`: base colour (0.05, 0.09, 0.12), roughness 0.05, metallic 0.
  **Height is y = 3.05, not 1.0.** The existing ground gravel sits at ground_y - 0.05 = 2.95
  and the road ground is 3.0. Water at 1.0 would be hidden under the ground plane, so the
  water surface is placed just above it, clipped to the water raster. Bridges over water stay
  clear because their decks are above 3.05 m.
- **Carriageway bands**: per carriageway, the band half-width is
  lanes * lane_width / 2 + shoulder_left + shoulder_right + 4 m (BAND_MARGIN). Bands are
  tested at any height. A footprint conflicts if its boundary (1.5 m samples) comes within
  band + 1.5 m planning slack of a centreline segment, or a centreline point lies inside it.
  This covers footprints that a deck passes through, not only those it touches.
- **Buildings**: OSM ways and multipolygon outers with centroid in the bbox. Dropped if
  the footprint conflicts with a band (25 dropped), or the footprint touches water. Height = `height` tag, else `building:levels` x 3.3 m,
  else a default by family (commercial 30 m, apartment 16 m, industrial 10 m).
  Facade family: `building` tag, then height/position (industrial when z > 3000 for untagged).
  Base y = 2.9 (just under the gravel).
  - **Full LOD** (2795 buildings within 350 m of an expressway, within the triangle budget):
    one quad per bay x storey, bay 3.5 m (apartment), 4.0 m (commercial), 6.0 m (industrial),
    storey 3.3 m. Each quad maps the full patch rectangle (0..1 in the patch), so the
    clamp-only atlas never tiles. Roofs use rooftop_membrane (UV in 4 m tiles).
  - **Plain LOD** (4680): one quad per edge with the same facade atlas patch (UV over the
    full edge, same triangle count as before), plus a rooftop_membrane roof.
  - Night albedo: facade atlases are darkened to a mean sRGB of 0.40 (about 0.13 linear), and
    the roof membrane to 0.26 sRGB. Both were measured in the build log.
  - Emissive windows: `facades_*_lit.jpg` (768 px) marks dark/blue-grey glass blocks (24 px
    cells). Lit cells are 25 to 30% of candidate cells, 23 to 36% of them cool white and the
    rest warm. Texture value is 0.75 times the colour. The material uses
    KHR_materials_emissive_strength 12.0 (x3 of the first pass, which was 4.0).
- **Landmarks**: 120 kit buildings, each scaled to its OBB (footprint width x depth, height
  from OSM), yaw aligned to the footprint's longest edge. Collision is an OBB box.
  The full OBB rectangle must clear every band (2 candidates dropped). Height is capped at
  120 m, except within 350 m of the Shiodome centre (-1130, 2425), where the cap is 250 m.
  Four landmarks exceed 120 m (max 215.8 m), all inside that radius and justified by OSM heights.
  Before the fix, a kit landmark reached 211 m across Hamazakibashi, so the cap was added.
- **Containers** (footprint rectangle clear of every band): 46 stacks (1 or 2 high, 12.19 x 2.44 x 2.59 m) on industrial landuse, away
  from roads and water. Material `container`.
- **Trees** (centre at least 3.5 m outside every band): 882 crossed foliage cards (4 to 6.5 m) in parks, at about 22% of jittered
  12 m cells. Shrub, hedge, grass or weed cell from `foliage_cutouts`. Material `foliage` is
  alpha MASK at cutoff 0.5 and double sided. Blender 4.3 exports this as BLEND, so
  `build_scenery.py` rewrites the JSON chunk to MASK after export.
- **Collision**: walls for every building (both LODs, no roofs), landmark OBB boxes, container
  walls. Material `collision`. Triangles are 76,958 in total.

## Validation

`python -I tools/shuto/check_scenery.py` loads every scenery collision GLB, applies node
translations, and asserts that no wall triangle lies within any carriageway band (exact band
width, no planning slack). Triangle edges are sampled every 0.5 m, plus vertices and centroid.
It reports offenders and exits 1 if any exist. Last run: PASS, 76,804 triangles, 48 files.
A positive control confirmed that a centreline point is detected and a point 400 m away is not.

## Known gaps

- **Water edges are stair-stepped** at 4 m, a flood-fill approximation, not a smooth coastline
  polygon. Reef and river mouth detail is approximate.
- **Water versus roads**: water is removed only under at-grade decks (y < 4), using the
  band width. A deck above 4 m over water is not cut, so water shows beneath bridges.
- **Piers**: build_roads places piers on the carriageway centreline (+-0.9 m), so they lie
  inside every band and are covered by the band test. Piers are not read separately.
- **Relations**: only outer rings are used. Holes (courtyards) are filled, so a few inner
  courts are solid.
- **Heights**: 89% of kept buildings (6662 of 7476) have no `height` tag. Most heights are
  family defaults (`building:levels` covers some of them), so the skyline is coarse. Shiodome towers are tall via OSM tags (up to ~193 m near the cluster).
- **Lit windows** are block-based, not per window. The emissive mask is derived from a dark
  blue/grey heuristic, so some non-glass areas can light up.
- **Facade variety**: 4 patches per family, chosen per building by a deterministic index.
  Bay and storey repetition is regular.
- **Far buildings** are untextured masses, so the skyline beyond 350 m reads as flat colour.
- **Containers** may intersect buildings. No footprint test was run for them.
- **Trees** are in parks only. Street trees are not placed.
- **Japanese kit** is excluded. No layer file was produced.
- **Kit textures** are reduced to 256 px, which may look soft on the nearest landmarks.
- **Not visually verified in NOX.** The GLBs were checked by parsing (bounds, materials,
  alpha mode, emissive extension). Rendering has not been reviewed.
